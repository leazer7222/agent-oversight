## Key decisions
- Use `tokens_in_hint` as the field name (not `prompt_chars`) — it's the right abstraction.
  The hint is a pre-call token count estimate, not raw char count. Features snapshot stores
  `prompt_chars = hint * 4` to remain schema-v1 compatible.
- p95 = 15x is deliberately aggressive when hint is missing — better to over-reserve than
  silently underrun. Phase 2 calibration will tighten this.
- Pre-fetch diff before `oversight.run()` — slight change to step timing, but we still emit
  step events for diff_retrieved and standards_loaded immediately after entering the context.
- claude-opus-4-5 and claude-opus-4-6 use same rates as claude-opus-4-7 ($15/$75 per M tokens)
