# Pre-Meeting Checklist - 1 hour before the Sprint Review

Human prep to walk in ready. The agent handles the data pull, authoring, and PDF;
these are the human touchpoints.

1. Review all current stories and bugs in the open sprint; be ready to close it in the meeting.
2. Carryover items: why did we not get to them? Separate external blockers from capacity.
3. Draft the next sprint's goal.
4. For items already in the next sprint: assign epics and assignees. (T-shirt sizes are set live
   in the meeting; bugs stay unsized and live in the Production Bugs bucket.)
5. Grade the closing sprint's GOAL part by part, with the why for each part. Goal-aware health:
   the goal being met is GREEN even at a low completion percentage. Do not judge on ticket count alone.
6. Know your velocity (completed-by-size) before committing the next sprint, and right-size the commit.
   Velocity is a BASELINE, not a trend, until roughly 5-6 fully-sized sprints. Do not over-commit the
   top end (M/L) beyond what recent sprints delivered.
7. Confirm the right work is IN the next sprint (carryover plus top backlog), not just cleanup.
8. Demo prep if stakeholders attend the review: line up the shipped work to show.
9. Retro page: keep it a structured page (Good / Bad / Ideas / Actions headings), not a whiteboard,
   and RE-PUBLISH after filling it in. Drafts are invisible to the API, so the agent cannot read them.
