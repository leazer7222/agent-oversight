## New lessons (also added to LESSONS_LEARNED if durable)
- The CCD session cache (`list_sessions` prNumber/prState/cwd) is UNRELIABLE - observed a chat cached
  `PR#2 CLOSED` that had actually shipped `PR#7 MERGED + deployed`. Always reconcile ship-state via live `gh`.
- Before archiving a project's chats, VERIFY the work is pushed to GitHub. Going project-by-project found
  two unbacked-up situations (unpushed commits; an entirely unversioned project) that "it shipped" hid.
- A project can span multiple cwd paths over its life (folder moves) - cwd is a lossy anchor; the
  feature/project is the durable unit, not the folder.
