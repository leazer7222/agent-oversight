## SAVED: Pre-meeting prep checklist (user wants to return to this for future cycles)
Human pre-meeting prep for a sprint review/planning cycle:
1. Review all current stories + bugs in the open sprint; prepare to close it in the meeting.
2. Carryover items - why did we not get to them? (external blockers vs capacity)
3. Draft the next sprint's goal.
4. For items already in the next sprint: assign epics + assignees (t-shirt sizes set live in the meeting).
5. Grade the closing sprint's GOAL part-by-part (goal-aware health = goal met is GREEN even at
   low % completion), with the why for each part - not just the ticket count.
6. Know your velocity (completed-by-size) BEFORE committing next sprint; right-size the commit.
   (Sprint 2 baseline: sized sample 21 = 10 XS, 6 S, 3 M, 2 Spike, no L+.)
7. Confirm the right work is IN the next sprint (carryover + top backlog), not just cleanup.
8. Demo prep if stakeholders attend the review.
9. Retro page: structured (Good/Bad/Ideas/Actions headings), not a whiteboard; RE-PUBLISH after
   filling (drafts are invisible to the API).
